Rockoon Battle

Launch your balloon from the hillside. Watch it climb and release its rocket. Can you fly through the gold ring?

Game

    ↻ Turn your phone sideways for a bigger view

    Aim—
    Power—

    How to play

    7 steps. Each one has its own link to share.

    1. Step 1Copy link

      Pick your crews

      Press “Play on this computer” and choose 2, 3 or 4 players. To play with friends on other screens, press “Play online” and share the 5-letter room code.

      Controls: Play on this computer234

    2. Step 2Copy link

      Choose your rockets

      Each crew can spend credits on rockets that scan wider around the ring. The Scout is free. Press Done when your crew is ready.

      Controls: BuyDone

    3. Step 3Copy link

      Check the wind

      The arrow at the top shows the wind. It carries your balloon sideways as it climbs, then bends your rocket the same way.

      Controls: Wind →

    4. Step 4Copy link

      Aim

      Drag from your balloon toward the gold ring, or press ◀ and ▶. The yellow dots show how your rocket will start.

      Controls: ◀▶

    5. Step 5Copy link

      Set the power

      Drag farther for more power, or press − and +. More power flies higher and farther.

      Controls: −+

    6. Step 6Copy link

      Launch

      Press Launch, or the Space key. Your balloon rises, then lets the rocket go.

      Controls: Launch

    7. Step 7Copy link

      Score

      Through the ring scores 3 points. Close to its edge scores 1. Each crew gets three launches a round, and the most points after the last round wins.

    Keyboard: Left and Right arrows aim. Up and Down set the power (Shift moves by 5). Q and E change the rocket. Space or Enter launches. P pauses. M turns sound on.

    Good to know

    • Each point earns 50 credits, and everyone starts with 600. A match has three rounds, or one per crew with four crews, so every crew launches once from every spot on the hill.
    • The barometer reads from pad to release. Altitudes are simulated metres above sea level, and pressure follows the standard atmosphere. Local weather can give a different reading.
    • Online: players get random names like GS-AB1. There is no chat.

    The shop

    • Scout: free, never runs out. Scan range beyond the ring: 12 pixels.
    • Tracker: 5 for 150 credits. Scan range beyond the ring: 20 pixels.
    • Mapper: 3 for 150 credits. Scan range beyond the ring: 28 pixels.
    • Surveyor: 2 for 250 credits. Scan range beyond the ring: 38 pixels.
    • Observatory: 1 for 300 credits. Scan range beyond the ring: 50 pixels.
