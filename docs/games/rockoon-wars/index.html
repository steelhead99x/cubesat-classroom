Rockoon Wars

Take turns launching rockets from balloons. Mind the wind, and be the last rockoon flying.

    How to play

    • Aim with a finger or the mouse: on your turn, press on the playfield and drag toward where you want to shoot. Farther from your rockoon means more power. The yellow dots show how your rocket starts.
    • Or use the buttons: ◀ ▶ change the angle and − + the power (hold to go faster). Tap the rocket to change it, then FIRE.
    • Keyboard: Left and Right arrows aim, Up and Down set power (Shift moves by 5), Q and E change rocket, Space or Enter fires, P pauses, M turns sound on (it starts off).
    • Hits earn credits, knocking a rockoon out earns a point, and the last one flying wins the round. Spend credits in the shop between rounds. Everyone starts with 600 credits.
    • Online: make a room and tell your friends its 5-letter code. Players get random names like GS-AB1. There is no chat.

    The shop

    • Baby Rocket: free, never runs out.
    • Rocket: 5 for 150 credits.
    • Big Bang: 2 for 400 credits.
    • Mega Bang: 1 for 900 credits.
    • Dirt Ball: 3 for 200 credits. Builds a hill instead of blowing one up.
