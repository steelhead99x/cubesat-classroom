Classroom games

Track a satellite on your own, or get friends together for a turn-based match.

Play in your browser with a keyboard, mouse or touch. No account required.

  • 🛰️

    Catch the Pass

    Follow a CubeSat across the sky, tune your radio and collect its data before it sets.

    • 👥 Solo levels or shared sky
    • ⏱ About 1 minute per pass
    ▶ Play Catch the Pass
  • 🎈

    Rockoon Battle

    Launch a balloon from the ground, release a rocket and score by flying through the sky ring.

    • 👥 2–4 players, local or online
    • ⏱ 5–15 minutes
    ▶ Play Rockoon Battle
