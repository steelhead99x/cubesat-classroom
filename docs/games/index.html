Games

Short games that teach how real satellite ground stations work. No account, no ads, no tracking.

  • Catch the Pass

    Aim a ground-station antenna at a CubeSat as it crosses the sky. Pick the right LoRa spreading factor, dodge 915 MHz noise and save the satellite’s battery to download every packet you can. Five levels, plus a live sky shared with other stations over Media over QUIC.

    Ages 8 and up · 1 minute a pass · keyboard, mouse or touch
