Print the 1U frame and payload

Four plates, about 3 h 50 min and 140 g of PLA with a 0.6 mm nozzle, supports off. No screws, nuts or metal standoffs. The frame is AMSAT's CubeSatSim v2.0 (GPL-3.0); the payload plate, strap and snap columns are new.

Fit requirements and evidence

The model targets a 100 × 100 × 113.5 mm envelope, 9 mm square rails, 3 mm end recesses, and 0.35 mm of locator clearance per side. Compare printed width at both ends and the middle, and record warping or support scars.

The meshes passed nominal size and integrity checks. Slicing, print measurements, positive plate retention, and deployer fit still need verification. Flight qualification requires review of the exact materials, process, and assembled hardware.
