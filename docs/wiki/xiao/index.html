Seeed Studio XIAO

A family of thumb-sized microcontroller boards from Seeed Studio.

Also called: Seeed XIAO, XIAO ESP32-C3, XIAO ESP32-S3, XIAO RP2040

XIAO is Seeed Studio’s family of very small microcontroller boards (about 21 × 17.5 mm), with versions built on chips such as the ESP32-C3, ESP32-S3, RP2040 and SAMD21. They can be programmed with the Arduino tools and are used here for the XIAO bench CubeSat.
