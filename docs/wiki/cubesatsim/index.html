CubeSatSim

AMSAT's low-cost CubeSat simulator for classrooms; this site's printed frame is forked from it.

Also called: CubeSat Simulator, AMSAT CubeSatSim

CubeSatSim, the AMSAT CubeSat Simulator, is an open-source, low-cost model of a CubeSat made for teaching. It has solar panels, batteries and a radio that sends telemetry like a real satellite, so students can practise receiving and decoding it. It was designed by Alan Johnston, KU2Y.

The printed frame on this site is a fork of the CubeSatSim v2.0 frame (GPL-3.0), with a new payload plate modeled on the Teachers in Space Multiplate.
