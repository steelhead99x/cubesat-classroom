Orbital debris mitigation

Plans that keep a satellite from leaving junk in orbit, such as deorbiting within 5 years.

Also called: debris plan, space debris, 5-year rule, deorbit

Orbital debris mitigation is the set of steps that keep a satellite from adding to space junk: limiting released parts, avoiding explosions, and removing the satellite from orbit after its mission. In 2022 the FCC adopted a “5-year rule”: satellites below 2,000 km that it authorizes, including amateur satellites under Part 97, must re-enter as soon as practicable and no later than five years after their mission ends. It applies to satellites launched after September 29, 2024. A debris plan is part of a satellite’s application.
