IARU frequency coordination

The process that assigns frequencies to amateur satellites so they don't interfere.

Also called: IARU, frequency coordination

The International Amateur Radio Union (IARU) runs satellite frequency coordination for satellites in the amateur-satellite service. A team describes its satellite, orbit and radio plan, and the IARU recommends frequencies that won’t clash with other satellites. Coordination comes alongside the satellite’s FCC license and international filing.
