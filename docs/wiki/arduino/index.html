Arduino

Open-source microcontroller boards and software, such as the Uno R3 and Nano 33 BLE Sense.

Also called: Uno R3, Arduino Uno, Nano 33 BLE Sense, Nano 33 BLE Sense Rev2

Arduino is an open-source electronics platform: small microcontroller boards plus free software for programming them. The Uno R3 is the classic beginner board, and the Nano 33 BLE Sense Rev2 packs temperature, humidity, pressure, light and motion sensors onto a small board. Both fit the printed payload plate on this site.
