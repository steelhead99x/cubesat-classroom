High-altitude balloon (HAB)

A large latex balloon that carries a small payload into the stratosphere.

Also called: HAB, weather balloon, small unmanned free balloon, free balloon

A high-altitude balloon (HAB) is a latex or plastic balloon, usually filled with helium or hydrogen, that lifts a small payload of sensors and cameras into the stratosphere, often above 25 km, before it bursts or is cut down and parachutes back. Under U.S. rules, small payloads under the weight limits in 14 CFR Part 101 need no FAA filing.
