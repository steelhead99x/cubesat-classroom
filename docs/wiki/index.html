Glossary

52 terms from the lessons, the hardware and the mission briefing, explained in plain language. Each entry lists other names for the term, related terms and its sources.

52 terms

#ABCDEFGHIKLMOPQRST

#

  • 1USpacecraft

    One CubeSat unit: about 10 × 10 × 11.35 cm.

    Also: one unit, 1U CubeSat, U (CubeSat unit)

  • 915 MHzRadio

    The center of the 902–928 MHz band, used by many LoRa radios in the Americas.

    Also: 915, US915, 33 cm band

A

  • Amateur radioRadio

    Licensed, non-commercial two-way radio, also called ham radio.

    Also: ham radio, amateur, call sign, callsign, licensed operator

  • Amateur-satellite band (435–438 MHz)Radio

    Radio frequencies set aside for amateur satellites, such as 435–438 MHz.

    Also: 70 cm band, 435 MHz, UHF amateur satellite band

  • AMSATOrganizations and programs

    The Radio Amateur Satellite Corporation, which builds amateur radio satellites and education tools.

    Also: Radio Amateur Satellite Corporation, AMSAT-NA

  • ApogeeLaunch and flight

    The highest point of an orbit or of a rocket's flight.

    Also: perigee, peak altitude

  • ArduinoHardware

    Open-source microcontroller boards and software, such as the Uno R3 and Nano 33 BLE Sense.

    Also: Uno R3, Arduino Uno, Nano 33 BLE Sense, Nano 33 BLE Sense Rev2

  • ARISSOrganizations and programs

    Amateur Radio on the ISS: school radio contacts with astronauts.

    Also: Amateur Radio on the International Space Station, SAREX, school contact

  • Artemis programOrganizations and programs

    NASA's program to return astronauts to the Moon.

    Also: Artemis, Moon program, Moon Base

B

  • Bench CubeSatHardware

    A CubeSat-style board kit that runs on a desk over USB, with no radio.

    Also: desk CubeSat, bench model

C

  • Commercial Lunar Payload Services (CLPS)Organizations and programs

    NASA's program that buys deliveries of science payloads to the Moon from companies.

    Also: CLPS, lunar delivery

  • CubeSatSpacecraft

    A small satellite built from standard 10 cm cube-shaped units.

    Also: nanosatellite, smallsat, cube satellite

  • CubeSat Design Specification (CDS)Spacecraft

    The published rules for CubeSat size, mass, rails and safety.

    Also: CDS, CubeSat standard

  • CubeSat Launch Initiative (CSLI)Organizations and programs

    NASA program that offers free launches to selected CubeSats from schools and nonprofits.

    Also: NASA CSLI, CubeSat Launch Initiative

  • CubeSatSimHardware

    AMSAT's low-cost CubeSat simulator for classrooms; this site's printed frame is forked from it.

    Also: CubeSat Simulator, AMSAT CubeSatSim

D

  • DeployerLaunch and flight

    The spring-loaded box that holds CubeSats during launch and pushes them into orbit.

    Also: dispenser, P-POD, J-SSOD, NRCSD, CubeSat deployer

  • DigipeaterRadio

    A digital repeater that receives and re-sends packet radio messages.

    Also: packet digipeater, packet radio, APRS digipeater

  • Downlink and uplinkRadio

    Downlink is space-to-ground radio; uplink is ground-to-space.

    Also: uplink, down link

E

  • Export controls (ITAR and EAR)Rules and safety

    U.S. rules that can limit sharing some spacecraft hardware and technical data.

    Also: ITAR, EAR, export control, USML

F

  • FAA Part 101Rules and safety

    U.S. rules for balloons, kites, amateur rockets and similar flights.

    Also: 14 CFR 101, Part 101, FAA rules

  • FCC Part 15Rules and safety

    U.S. rules that let certified low-power radios run without a license.

    Also: Part 15, unlicensed radio, 15.247

  • FCC Part 97Rules and safety

    U.S. rules for the Amateur Radio Service, which needs a licensed operator.

    Also: Part 97, amateur rules, 97.119, 97.207

  • Forward error correction (FEC)Software and data

    Extra data sent with a message so the receiver can repair losses without asking again.

    Also: FEC, error correction, erasure coding

G

  • Ground stationSoftware and data

    The antenna, radio and computer that talk to a spacecraft from Earth.

    Also: ground segment, pass, satellite pass, receiver

H

  • High-altitude balloon (HAB)Launch and flight

    A large latex balloon that carries a small payload into the stratosphere.

    Also: HAB, weather balloon, small unmanned free balloon, free balloon

  • High-power rocket (Class 2)Rules and safety

    A larger amateur rocket that needs FAA authorization and air-traffic notice.

    Also: Class 2 rocket, HPR, high power rocketry

I

  • IARU frequency coordinationRules and safety

    The process that assigns frequencies to amateur satellites so they don't interfere.

    Also: IARU, frequency coordination

  • International Space Station (ISS)Organizations and programs

    The crewed orbiting laboratory about 400 km above Earth.

    Also: ISS, space station

  • ISM band (902–928 MHz)Radio

    Radio bands shared by industrial, scientific and unlicensed devices, such as 902–928 MHz.

    Also: ISM, 902-928 MHz, unlicensed band

K

  • Kármán lineLaunch and flight

    The 100 km altitude commonly used as the edge of space.

    Also: edge of space, 100 km

L

  • LoRaRadio

    A long-range, low-power radio modulation used by sensors and trackers.

    Also: LoRaWAN, SX1276, LoRa board

  • Low Earth orbit (LEO)Launch and flight

    Orbits close to Earth, up to about 2,000 km high, where most CubeSats fly.

    Also: LEO, orbit, orbital velocity, low orbit

M

  • Media over QUIC (MoQ)Software and data

    An IETF protocol for live media and data that fans out through relays.

    Also: MoQ, Media over QUIC, MoQ relay, track, fan-out

  • MicrogravityLaunch and flight

    The near-weightless condition of objects in free fall, such as in orbit.

    Also: weightlessness, zero-g, zero gravity

  • Model rocket (Class 1)Rules and safety

    A small, low-power rocket that U.S. rules let you fly without FAA filing.

    Also: Class 1 rocket, Class 1 model rocket

O

  • Orbital debris mitigationRules and safety

    Plans that keep a satellite from leaving junk in orbit, such as deorbiting within 5 years.

    Also: debris plan, space debris, 5-year rule, deorbit

P

  • PayloadSpacecraft

    The part of a spacecraft or flight that does the actual mission, such as sensors or an experiment.

    Also: experiment, instrument, payload plate

  • PLA (polylactic acid)Hardware

    The common, easy-to-print plastic used for this site's printed frame.

    Also: polylactic acid, 3D printing filament, ASA

Q

  • QUICSoftware and data

    A fast, encrypted internet transport protocol that HTTP/3 and MoQ run on.

    Also: RFC 9000

R

  • Raspberry PiHardware

    A small, low-cost single-board computer.

    Also: Pi, Raspberry Pi 2

  • Rideshare launchLaunch and flight

    Many satellites sharing one rocket to split the cost.

    Also: ride share, rideshare mission

  • RockoonLaunch and flight

    A rocket carried up by a balloon and launched high in the atmosphere.

    Also: balloon-launched rocket, rocket balloon

  • RSSI and SNRRadio

    Two measures of signal quality: received strength and signal-to-noise ratio.

    Also: RSSI, SNR, signal strength, dBm

S

  • Satellite busSpacecraft

    The spacecraft systems that keep a satellite alive: structure, power, computer, radio.

    Also: bus, platform, 1U platform

  • SatNOGSOrganizations and programs

    An open-source, worldwide network of volunteer satellite ground stations.

    Also: Satellite Networked Open Ground Station, Libre Space Foundation

  • Seeed Studio XIAOHardware

    A family of thumb-sized microcontroller boards from Seeed Studio.

    Also: Seeed XIAO, XIAO ESP32-C3, XIAO ESP32-S3, XIAO RP2040

  • Sub-GHz radioRadio

    Radio below 1 GHz: slower than Wi-Fi, but it reaches farther on less power.

    Also: sub-gigahertz, below 1 GHz

  • Suborbital flightLaunch and flight

    A flight that reaches space or high altitude but falls back without orbiting.

    Also: sounding rocket, research flight, suborbital

  • Sun-synchronous orbit (SSO)Launch and flight

    A near-polar orbit that passes over each place at the same local time.

    Also: SSO, sun synchronous

T

  • Teacher in Space ProjectOrganizations and programs

    NASA's 1984 program to fly a schoolteacher in space.

    Also: Teacher in Space, NASA Teacher in Space

  • Teachers in Space (TIS)Organizations and programs

    A nonprofit that trains teachers to build Arduino CubeSats and fly them.

    Also: TIS, tis.org

  • TelemetrySoftware and data

    Measurements a vehicle sends home by radio, such as temperature and battery voltage.

    Also: housekeeping data, telemetry.v1, TLM

How to contribute

This glossary is a wiki: anyone can improve it on GitHub. Each entry is one Markdown file in site/src/content/glossary, and every page has an "Edit this page on GitHub" link that opens a pull request. Keep definitions short and plain, cite a source for any technical or regulatory fact, and link related terms by their file names. See the contribution guide.
