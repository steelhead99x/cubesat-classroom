CubeSat Design Specification (CDS)

The published rules for CubeSat size, mass, rails and safety.

Also called: CDS, CubeSat standard

The CubeSat Design Specification (CDS) is the document, published by Cal Poly’s CubeSat program, that says what a CubeSat must be: outside dimensions, mass, rail size and smoothness, how far parts may stick out, and safety rules such as deployment switches. Launch providers and deployers build around it, so meeting the CDS is how a CubeSat earns a ride.

The current version used here is Rev 14.1 (2022).
