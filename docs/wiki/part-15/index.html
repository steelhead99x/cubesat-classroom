FCC Part 15

U.S. rules that let certified low-power radios run without a license.

Also called: Part 15, unlicensed radio, 15.247

47 CFR Part 15 is the FCC rule for radio devices that can be used without a license, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and many LoRa modules. In exchange, a Part 15 device must not cause harmful interference and must accept interference from others. Sections such as §15.247 set power and modulation limits in bands including 902–928 MHz.

Using a certified module as certified is the simple path; changing its antenna or power can take it outside its certification.
