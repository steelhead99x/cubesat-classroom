Terms

These terms describe how we ask you to use the CubeSat Classroom website. They are written in plain language and are not legal advice.

Last updated 27 November 2026.

Who runs this site

This site is operated by the CubeSat Classroom volunteer project, an independent open-source space education project. Its public home is cubesatclass.org. The source for the site and the lessons is the public GitHub repository github.com/steelhead99x/cubesat-classroom.

For questions about these terms, open an issue on that GitHub repository. That is the contact path this page uses.

Who may use the site

The site is for learners 13 and up, and for the teachers, guardians and mentors who guide them. It is not directed at children under 13. Younger learners may take part only alongside an adult who stays for the whole activity and never shares a login with anyone under 13.

What you will find here

The site offers free lessons, practice tools, games and printable hardware files for learning about satellites and radio. It is an educational resource. It is not a substitute for a licence, a safety review, or professional engineering or legal advice. Flight hosts, radio regulators and your own mentors decide what is allowed in a real activity.

Open-source materials

Unless a file says otherwise, the project's code and documentation are offered under the MIT or Apache 2.0 licences, at your choice. Those licences are in the repository and are the terms that govern reuse of the materials. If you share work back, follow the project's contributing guide, including its rules about student privacy and attribution.

Radio and safety

Transmitting on amateur radio bands requires a licensed control operator. Students may take part under that operator. Do not use this site to get around radio rules, aviation rules, or the safety instructions in the lessons.

No warranty

The site and the materials are provided as is, without a promise that they are complete, current, or fit for a particular purpose. Live games and optional tools may be unavailable from time to time. Links to other websites are provided for convenience; those sites have their own terms.

Changes

If these terms change, we will update the date above. The current version will always be at this address. The privacy page explains how the site handles information.
