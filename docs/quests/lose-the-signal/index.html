Quest 3 of 5, 10 minutes, communication loss

Lose the signal on purpose

Pause contact in the middle of a video burst and see what the ground keeps.

Pick your level

Steps for beginner crews

  1. Press Start video.
  2. After two or three pictures, press Pause contact. This is like the satellite flying out of sight.
  3. Look at the mission log. How many pictures made it?
  4. Press Restore contact, then ask for a new reading.

Check your work

You know how many pictures arrived before contact stopped, and what came back afterwards.

Talk it over

A real satellite is only overhead for a few minutes. What would you do first when it comes back into view?

Steps for intermediate crews

  1. Start a video burst and pause contact after two or three frames.
  2. Record how many frames arrived and how the station marks the burst.
  3. Restore contact and send a sensors request. Record how long it takes to recover.

Check your work

Your log shows a partial burst marked incomplete and a successful request after recovery.

Talk it over

Should the ground station throw away an incomplete burst or keep it? Give one reason each way.

Steps for professional crews

  1. Start a burst and pause contact mid-group. Note the last frame index received before the group stalls.
  2. Restore contact and issue a new request. Confirm the new reply carries a new request ID.
  3. Explain why the station deduplicates request IDs and expires commands after 5 seconds.

Check your work

You identified the stall point, the recovery request ID and the purpose of the 5 s expiry.

Talk it over

The real station rejects commands older than 5 s. What could go wrong if a delayed command ran after contact returned?
