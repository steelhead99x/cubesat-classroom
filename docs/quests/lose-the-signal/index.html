Lesson 3 of 11 · 10 min · Communication loss

Lose the signal on purpose

Pause contact in the middle of a video burst and see what the ground keeps.

Pick your level

On paper, write the lesson title and your level. Number your notes to match the steps. Tick each one when you finish it.

Steps for beginner crews

  1. Press Take pictures.Copy link · copied
  2. When a few pictures have arrived, press Pause contact. This is like the satellite flying out of sight.Copy link · copied
  3. Look at the mission log. How many pictures made it?Copy link · copied
  4. Press Restore contact, then ask for a new reading.Copy link · copied

Check your work

You know how many pictures arrived before contact stopped, and what came back afterwards.

Copy link · copied

Talk it over

A real satellite is only overhead for a few minutes. What would you do first when it comes back into view?

Copy link · copied

Steps for intermediate crews

  1. Start a video burst and pause contact after two or three frames.Copy link · copied
  2. Record how many frames arrived and how the station marks the burst.Copy link · copied
  3. Restore contact and send a sensors request. Record how long it takes to recover.Copy link · copied

Check your work

Your log shows a partial burst marked incomplete and a successful request after recovery.

Copy link · copied

Talk it over

Should the ground station throw away an incomplete burst or keep it? Give one reason each way.

Copy link · copied

Steps for professional crews

  1. Start a burst and pause contact mid-group. Note the last frame index received before the group stalls.Copy link · copied
  2. Restore contact and issue a new request. Confirm the reply line shows a new request id.Copy link · copied
  3. Explain why the station deduplicates request IDs and expires commands after 5 seconds.Copy link · copied

Check your work

You identified the stall point, the recovery request ID and the purpose of the 5 s expiry.

Copy link · copied

Talk it over

The real station rejects commands older than 5 s. What could go wrong if a delayed command ran after contact returned?

Copy link · copied
