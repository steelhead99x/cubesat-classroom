Lesson 7 of 11 · 15 min · Prediction and correction

Rockoon Battle

Launch a rocket from a balloon, see how the wind moves it, and adjust your aim before the next shot.

Pick your level

On paper, write the lesson title and your level. Number your notes to match the steps. Tick each one when you finish it.

Steps for beginner crews

  1. Open the game, press Play on this computer and pick 2 crews.Copy link · copied
  2. Before each launch, look at the wind arrow at the top. Write down which way it points.Copy link · copied
  3. Launch. Did your rocket land on the side the wind was blowing toward? Change your aim and try again.Copy link · copied

Check your work

You wrote down the wind for at least three launches and what you changed each time.

Copy link · copied

Talk it over

The balloon carries the rocket before it launches. How does the wind move your rocket twice?

Copy link · copied

Steps for intermediate crews

  1. Pick one crew and a round with wind. For that crew’s first launch, aim straight at the ring and record the wind, angle, power, rocket and score (3, 1 or 0).Copy link · copied
  2. Use the same power and rocket for that crew’s two remaining launches. Change only the angle to aim a little upwind.Copy link · copied
  3. Compare the three scores. If the launch pad shifted during the round, note that before comparing.Copy link · copied

Check your work

Your table has three launches by the same crew in one round, and one sentence about what changed when you aimed upwind.

Copy link · copied

Talk it over

Real rockoon teams wait for low winds before they launch. Using your table, explain why.

Copy link · copied

Steps for professional crews

  1. Read balloonPosition, releasePoint and WIND_ACCEL in site/src/games/rockoon-wars/logic.mjs. For a wind of 10, work out how far sideways the balloon drifts before release, in game pixels.Copy link · copied
  2. Compare the dashed balloon path with your calculated drift. Check a second round with a different wind. Record the wind strength and direction for each observation.Copy link · copied
  3. Record the simulated air pressure at the pad and at release. Describe how it changed as the balloon climbed.Copy link · copied

Check your work

You have the drift from the code with working, two wind observations, and a sentence about the pressure difference.

Copy link · copied

Talk it over

The game rules avoid `Math.pow` and use a seeded random number generator so every screen in an online room computes the same flight. Why does that matter when one computer replays another's moves?

Copy link · copied
