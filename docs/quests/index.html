Classroom lessons

11 lessons, about 120 minutes in all. Lessons 1 to 5 use the practice ground station on each lesson page. The others open a game, the receiver pedal, the rocket workbook or cubesat.app in a new tab.

Pick your level

13 and up and new to satellites, or anyone new

Middle and high school, or new radio operators

Advanced students, mentors and engineers

  1. 01
    Make first contact

    Ask the practice CubeSat for a sensor reading and record it with its units.

    5 min · Measurement and units
    Practice ground station

  2. 02
    Bring back a picture

    Start the test camera and count how many frames reach the ground.

    5 min · Data return
    Practice ground station

  3. 03
    Lose the signal on purpose

    Pause contact in the middle of a video burst and see what the ground keeps.

    10 min · Communication loss
    Practice ground station

  4. 04
    Fresh or historical?

    Find out when a reading stops counting as current, and why the station cares.

    5 min · Data quality
    Practice ground station

  5. 05
    Light and shadow

    Take readings over a minute and explain why the light level sometimes drops to zero.

    10 min · Patterns in data
    Practice ground station

  6. 06
    Catch the Pass

    Point a ground antenna at a passing satellite and choose a radio setting that still works when it is low.

    15 min · Tracking and radio links
    Catch the Pass

  7. 07
    Rockoon Battle

    Launch a rocket from a balloon, see how the wind moves it, and adjust your aim before the next shot.

    15 min · Prediction and correction
    Rockoon Battle

  8. 08
    Follow the Doppler shift

    Tune the CSC-R1P receiver pedal, then keep a passing satellite's signal in the passband as its frequency drifts.

    15 min · Tuning and Doppler shift
    Satellite pedal trainer

  9. 09
    Predict a space station pass

    Use orbit data to find when the International Space Station next crosses your sky, and where to look.

    10 min · Orbits and timing
    Passes on cubesat.app

  10. 10
    Who may transmit?

    Use the rule finder on cubesat.app to see how the same 915 MHz radio can be allowed, need a licence, or not be allowed.

    10 min · Radio rules
    Bands on cubesat.app

  11. 11
    Predict a rocket flight

    Enter what your crew measured, pick a certified motor, and get a prediction to test at a club launch.

    20 min · Modelling and measurement
    Rocket flight workbook
