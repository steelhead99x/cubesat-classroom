Five quests. Five patches. About 35 minutes.

Every quest runs in the practice ground station, so all you need is this page. Pick a level first. You can change it any time.

Pick your level

Beginner

Ages 9 and up, or anyone new to satellites

A ground station is a computer on Earth that talks to a satellite. You ask a question, wait, and read the answer. Plain words, hints along the way.

Intermediate

Middle and high school, or new radio operators

Requests and replies take time, data can arrive incomplete, and old data can mislead you. You will record timing, units and sequence numbers.

Professional

Advanced students, mentors and engineers

See the station the way its software does: MoQ broadcasts and tracks, groups and frames, the raw telemetry.v1 JSON and round-trip times.

  1. Quest 1, 5 minutes

    Make first contact

    Ask the practice CubeSat for a sensor reading and record it with its units.

    Measurement and units

  2. Quest 2, 5 minutes

    Bring back a picture

    Start the test camera and count how many frames reach the ground.

    Data return

  3. Quest 3, 10 minutes

    Lose the signal on purpose

    Pause contact in the middle of a video burst and see what the ground keeps.

    Communication loss

  4. Quest 4, 5 minutes

    Fresh or historical?

    Find out when a reading stops counting as current, and why the station cares.

    Data quality

  5. Quest 5, 10 minutes

    Light and shadow

    Take readings over a minute and explain why the light level sometimes drops to zero.

    Patterns in data
