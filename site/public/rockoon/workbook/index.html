What this is. A desk simulation: a point-mass flight with a simple drag model, rail friction and the shear clip, and stability by the Barrowman equations (nose and fins). Check the stability in OpenRocket too; a mentor will ask. Every number is a prediction to compare with your flights, not a measurement.

Rules of thumb used: stability at least 1 calibre (body diameter) and not over about 3; liftoff thrust-to-weight at least 5:1; rail exit at least 15 m/s. They are guidance, not regulations: your club’s range safety officer has the final word.

Safety. Certified commercial motors only, installed by a certified adult, flown at a club launch under the NAR Model Rocket Safety Code. Students never handle live motors or igniters.
