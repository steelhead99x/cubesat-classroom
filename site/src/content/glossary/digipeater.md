A **digipeater** (digital repeater) receives short packet radio messages and sends them on again, so two stations too far apart to hear each other can connect. The [International Space Station](../iss/) has carried an amateur radio packet digipeater, letting ground stations hundreds or thousands of kilometres apart relay messages through it.
