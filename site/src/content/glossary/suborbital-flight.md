A **suborbital flight** goes up, often into space, and comes back down without enough sideways speed to [orbit](../leo/). Sounding rockets, rockoons and crewed spaceplanes make suborbital flights, giving a few minutes of [microgravity](../microgravity/) at much lower cost than orbit.
