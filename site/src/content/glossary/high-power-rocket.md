A **high-power rocket** is an amateur rocket bigger than a model rocket. Under FAA rules a Class 2 high-power rocket has a total motor impulse of up to 40,960 N·s; flying one requires FAA authorization (often a waiver held by a launch club) and notice to air traffic control (§101.25 and §101.27). Rocketry clubs also require flyers to hold a high-power certification.
