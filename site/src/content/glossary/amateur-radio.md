**Amateur radio** (ham radio) is a licensed radio service for non-commercial communication, experimenting and learning. Operators pass an exam and receive a **call sign**. Amateurs build and use satellites, talk with astronauts through [ARISS](../ariss/), and help in emergencies. In the U.S. the rules are [FCC Part 97](../part-97/). In CubeSat Classroom, amateur bands are receive-only unless a licensed operator is in control.
