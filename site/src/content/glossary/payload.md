The **payload** is whatever a flight is for: a camera, sensors, a science experiment or a radio. Everything else (the frame, power, computer and radio that keep the spacecraft working) is the [bus](../satellite-bus/).

In CubeSat Classroom, the printed payload plate holds an Arduino Uno R3 and a Nano 33 BLE Sense, which are the classroom payload.
