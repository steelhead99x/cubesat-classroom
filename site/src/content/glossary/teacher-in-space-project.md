The **Teacher in Space Project** was announced by President Reagan on August 27, 1984, to send a schoolteacher into space as the first citizen passenger. Christa McAuliffe was selected, with Barbara Morgan as her backup. McAuliffe died with her crewmates when Space Shuttle Challenger was lost on January 28, 1986. Barbara Morgan later flew to the International Space Station on STS-118 in 2007.
