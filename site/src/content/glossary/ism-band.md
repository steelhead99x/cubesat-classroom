**ISM bands** (industrial, scientific and medical) are frequency ranges where many unlicensed devices share the air. In the Americas, **902–928 MHz** is one of them, and certified low-power radios can use it under [FCC Part 15](../part-15/). Other regions use different ranges: Europe's similar band is around 868 MHz.
