NASA's **CubeSat Launch Initiative (CSLI)** gives selected CubeSats from U.S. educational institutions, nonprofits and NASA centers a ride to space at no launch cost, on rockets that are already flying. Teams apply through a public call, and selected teams still build, test and license their own satellite.
