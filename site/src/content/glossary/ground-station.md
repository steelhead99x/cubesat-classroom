A **ground station** is the equipment on Earth that receives a spacecraft's [downlink](../downlink/), and sometimes sends commands: an antenna, a radio receiver and a computer to decode the data. A satellite in [low Earth orbit](../leo/) is only in view for a few minutes at a time, called a **pass**, so networks of stations such as [SatNOGS](../satnogs/) share the work.

CubeSat Classroom's practice ground station runs in the browser.
