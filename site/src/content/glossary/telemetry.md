**Telemetry** is data a spacecraft, balloon or rocket measures and sends to the ground: temperature, battery voltage, light, rotation, position and signal strength. Ground teams use it to check the vehicle's health and to do science.

CubeSat Classroom's telemetry track is called `telemetry.v1` and carries values such as `temperature_c`, `bus_voltage_v`, `light_lux` and `gyro_dps`.
