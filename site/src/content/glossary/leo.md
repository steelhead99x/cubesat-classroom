**Low Earth orbit (LEO)** is the region of orbits close to Earth, up to about 2,000 km altitude. The [International Space Station](../iss/) and most CubeSats are in LEO, a few hundred kilometres up.

To stay in orbit there, a spacecraft must travel sideways at about 7.8 km/s; that is why reaching space (going up) is much easier than reaching orbit (going fast). A satellite in LEO goes around Earth in about 90 minutes, so a ground station only hears it for a few minutes per [pass](../ground-station/).
