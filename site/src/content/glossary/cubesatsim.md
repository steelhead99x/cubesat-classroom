**CubeSatSim**, the AMSAT CubeSat Simulator, is an open-source, low-cost model of a CubeSat made for teaching. It has solar panels, batteries and a radio that sends [telemetry](../telemetry/) like a real satellite, so students can practise receiving and decoding it. It was designed by Alan Johnston, KU2Y.

The printed frame on this site is a fork of the CubeSatSim v2.0 frame (GPL-3.0), with a new payload plate modeled on the [Teachers in Space](../teachers-in-space/) Multiplate.
