**PLA** (polylactic acid) is the most common 3D-printing plastic. It prints easily at low temperature with little warping, which makes it good for classroom parts such as the printed CubeSat frame and test coupons. It softens at fairly low temperatures, so flight parts that get hot use other materials, such as ASA.
