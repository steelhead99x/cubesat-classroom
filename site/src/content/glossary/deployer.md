A **deployer** is the container that carries CubeSats on a rocket or space station and then pushes them out, usually with a spring, once they reach the right place. The satellite slides out along its corner rails, which is why the [CubeSat Design Specification](../cubesat-design-specification/) is strict about rails.

Examples include Cal Poly's original P-POD and JAXA's J-SSOD, which releases CubeSats from the [International Space Station](../iss/).
