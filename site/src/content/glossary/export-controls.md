**Export controls** are U.S. rules on sending certain items and technical data to other countries or foreign persons. The **ITAR** (State Department) covers defense articles on the U.S. Munitions List, including some spacecraft items (22 CFR Part 121). The **EAR** (Commerce Department) covers many other items, including much commercial and research spacecraft hardware.

Educational CubeSat projects that publish open designs should still check whether any part, software or data they plan to share is controlled. This entry is not legal advice.
