A **rockoon** (rocket + balloon) is a small rocket lifted by a high-altitude balloon and fired once the balloon is above most of the air. Starting high means far less drag, so a small rocket can climb much higher than it could from the ground. Rockoons were used for research in the 1950s and 1960s; a Sigma-4 rockoon reached 105 km in 1961.

Rockoons can reach toward space, but not [orbit](../leo/). CubeSat Classroom's rockoon work is a concept in development; no flights have been made, and experienced adults would lead every launch.
