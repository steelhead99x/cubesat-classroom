**1U** means one CubeSat unit. The CubeSat Design Specification sets a 1U CubeSat at 100 × 100 × 113.5 mm, including its corner rails, with a mass limit of 2.00 kg in Rev 14.1. A 3U is three units long, a 6U is two 3Us side by side, and so on.

The printed frame on this site is a 1U frame.
