**Teachers in Space (TIS)** is a nonprofit that trains teachers in space science and engineering. Its workshops teach educators to build Arduino CubeSats and fly them on balloons, gliders and rockets, and it works to send teachers on research flights. Its name carries forward NASA's 1984 [Teacher in Space Project](../teacher-in-space-project/).

The payload plate on this site's printed frame is modeled on the TIS 1U frame's Multiplate. CubeSat Classroom is independent and not endorsed by TIS.
