A CubeSat is a small satellite built to a shared standard size. The basic unit, called **1U**, is a cube about 10 cm on each side; bigger CubeSats join units together (2U, 3U, 6U and so on).

Because every CubeSat has the same outside shape, it can ride to space in a standard [deployer](../deployer/) alongside other satellites. That makes launches cheaper, so schools, universities and small teams can fly real spacecraft. The standard was created by Cal Poly and Stanford in 1999 and is kept up to date as the [CubeSat Design Specification](../cubesat-design-specification/).
