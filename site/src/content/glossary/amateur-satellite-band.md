The **amateur-satellite service** has its own frequency segments. A common downlink segment for small satellites is **435–438 MHz** in the 70 cm band, listed for space stations in [§97.207](../part-97/). Satellites using it must coordinate their frequency through the [IARU](../iaru-coordination/).

A student satellite would send open, identified [telemetry](../telemetry/) in this band, and classrooms would only receive it.
