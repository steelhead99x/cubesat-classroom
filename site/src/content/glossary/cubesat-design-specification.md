The **CubeSat Design Specification (CDS)** is the document, published by Cal Poly's CubeSat program, that says what a CubeSat must be: outside dimensions, mass, rail size and smoothness, how far parts may stick out, and safety rules such as deployment switches. Launch providers and [deployers](../deployer/) build around it, so meeting the CDS is how a CubeSat earns a ride.

The current version used here is Rev 14.1 (2022).
