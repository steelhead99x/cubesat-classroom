**47 CFR Part 97** is the FCC rule for the [Amateur Radio Service](../amateur-radio/). Transmitting under Part 97 needs a licensed control operator. Among other rules, a station must identify with its call sign at least every 10 minutes and at the end of a contact (§97.119), and messages may not be encoded to hide their meaning. Section §97.207 lists the bands amateur satellites (space stations) may use, including [435–438 MHz](../amateur-satellite-band/).
