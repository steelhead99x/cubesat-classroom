**AMSAT** (the Radio Amateur Satellite Corporation) is a volunteer nonprofit of radio amateurs founded in 1969. It designs and builds amateur radio satellites, supports satellite operators, and runs education work such as [CubeSatSim](../cubesatsim/).

CubeSat Classroom is an independent project and is not made or endorsed by AMSAT.
