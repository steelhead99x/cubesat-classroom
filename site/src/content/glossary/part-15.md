**47 CFR Part 15** is the FCC rule for radio devices that can be used without a license, such as Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and many [LoRa](../lora/) modules. In exchange, a Part 15 device must not cause harmful interference and must accept interference from others. Sections such as §15.247 set power and modulation limits in bands including [902–928 MHz](../ism-band/).

Using a certified module as certified is the simple path; changing its antenna or power can take it outside its certification.
