A **high-altitude balloon (HAB)** is a latex or plastic balloon, usually filled with helium or hydrogen, that lifts a small payload of sensors and cameras into the stratosphere, often above 25 km, before it bursts or is cut down and parachutes back. Under U.S. rules, small payloads under the weight limits in [14 CFR Part 101](../faa-part-101/) need no FAA filing.
