The **International Space Station (ISS)** is a crewed laboratory in [low Earth orbit](../leo/), about 400 km up, built and run by space agencies of the United States, Russia, Europe, Japan and Canada. It has had people aboard continuously since November 2000. Some CubeSats are released into orbit from the ISS through a [deployer](../deployer/).
