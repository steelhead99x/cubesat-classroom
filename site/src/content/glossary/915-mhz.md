**915 MHz** is shorthand for the 902–928 MHz [ISM band](../ism-band/) used in the Americas, named after its middle. [LoRa](../lora/) radios and other sub-GHz devices sold for the U.S. are often called 915 MHz versions. CubeSat Classroom's bench radio tests, including live video, run at 915 MHz under [FCC Part 15](../part-15/).
