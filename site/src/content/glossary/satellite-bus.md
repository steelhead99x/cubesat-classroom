A **satellite bus** is the support part of a spacecraft: the structure, power system (solar cells and batteries), flight computer, attitude control and radio. The bus carries and serves the [payload](../payload/). Companies sell ready-made CubeSat buses, often called platforms, so a team can focus on its payload.
