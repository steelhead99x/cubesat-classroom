The **Kármán line** is an altitude of 100 km, widely used as the boundary between Earth's atmosphere and outer space; the World Air Sports Federation (FAI) uses it for astronautics records. Some U.S. agencies use 50 miles (about 80 km) instead. Crossing it means reaching space, but not [orbit](../leo/).
