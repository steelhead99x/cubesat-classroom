A **bench CubeSat** is a classroom build that behaves like a CubeSat on a desk: a microcontroller with sensors that "deploys", records readings, loses the signal and sends the data back, all over USB serial with no radio. It lets a class practise mission operations before anything flies. CubeSat Classroom has Arduino and XIAO versions.
