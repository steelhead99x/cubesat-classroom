**Sub-GHz** radio uses frequencies below 1 GHz, such as [915 MHz](../915-mhz/) or 433 MHz. Compared with 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi it carries less data, but its signals travel farther and pass through walls and trees better for the same power, which suits sensors, trackers and small satellites.
