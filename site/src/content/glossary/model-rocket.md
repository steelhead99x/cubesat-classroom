A **model rocket** is a small rocket with a factory-made motor. Under FAA rules a Class 1 model rocket uses no more than 125 g of propellant, weighs no more than 1,500 g including the motor, and is launched from the ground; it needs no FAA filing when flown within §101.22(a) limits. Hobby groups such as the National Association of Rocketry publish safety codes for them.
