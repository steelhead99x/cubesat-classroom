# [S] Print the Rev A shell and lid in PLA

## Goal

Print and identify a matching shell and lid whose settings and visible defects can be reproduced.

## Plan

Print the [case shell](/hardware/p1s/02-case-shell.3mf) and [camera lid](/hardware/p1s/03-camera-lid.3mf) using the reviewed orientation and coupon process. Record any change to print settings and why it was needed. After cooling, record exterior dimensions, corner lift, and any cracking or damaged tabs. Photograph the mating faces and record which shell and lid were paired. Record a failed print with its preset and visible defect before retrying.

## Required

- [PLA case coupon](/quest/m0/print-p1s-case-coupon.md) - verifies the fastener fit before a full print.
