# [S] Mentor: help a learner lead the next demo

## Goal

A learner who completed a mentor demo leads the same demo for a new group, while the mentor stays the whole time and steps in only when needed. Experience grows at every level: the learner becomes a guide, and the mentor learns what to explain better.

## Plan

Follow the [mentor rules](/quest/m0/mentor-get-ready.md#mentor-rules). The mentor is still responsible for safety, keeps every login, and stays from start to finish.

1. **Choose (mentor and learner).** Pick a demo the learner has done, such as the [ground station demo](/quest/m0/mentor-ground-station-demo.md). The learner reads the steps and marks the parts they want help with.
2. **Practise (learner, mentor watching).** The learner runs the demo once with the mentor as the only audience.
3. **Lead (learner).** The learner guides a new group through every step. The mentor watches, checks wiring or safety steps, and helps only when asked or when something is unsafe.
4. **Talk it over (together).** The learner says what went well and one thing to change. The mentor adds one observation.
5. **Log (mentor).** Record the demo, the learner's crew nickname, how many in the new group did every step, and the change you agreed on. Share the improved instruction through the [contribution guide](/CONTRIBUTING.md).

### Check your work

The new group did every step, the mentor stayed the whole time, and the instructions are better for the next crew.

## Required

- [Mentor: guide a first ground station demo](/quest/m0/mentor-ground-station-demo.md) - the learner has done a mentor demo before leading one.
