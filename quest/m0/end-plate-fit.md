# [S] Verify both printed end plates seat

## Goal

Record whether the base and camera plates seat by hand with their locators inward and stay within the Rev B rail envelope.

## Plan

Use the same material and process as the frame. Measure seating, rocking and end recess. Record any clearance adjustment. Keep the slip-fit result distinct from the positive retention test.

## Required

- [Full printed rail frame](/quest/m0/full-frame-fit.md) - provides a measured mating article.
