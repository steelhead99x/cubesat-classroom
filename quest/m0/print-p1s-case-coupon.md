# [S] Print and measure the PLA case coupon

## Goal

Check that your screws fit before spending four hours on the shell.

## Plan

Print the [M3 fit coupon](/hardware/p1s/01-m3-fit-coupon.3mf). From the notched end the holes are 2.6, 2.8, 3.0, 3.2, 3.5 and 3.8 mm. Using one of your M3 × 12 screws, record which holes it threads into without cracking and which it slides through. The shell uses 2.8 mm and the lid 3.5 mm. If either fails, record the size that worked and follow [If the screw doesn't fit](/hardware/p1s/README.md#if-the-screw-doesnt-fit).

## Required

- [P1S case slice review](/quest/m0/slice-p1s-case.md) - provides the slicer settings.
