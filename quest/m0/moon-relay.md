# [S] Send a reading from the Moon

## Goal

Deliver a pretend Moon sensor reading to an Earth ground station after a break in contact. Explain how the station can tell when the reading was taken.

## Plan

1. Pick three roles. One student is a sensor on the Moon, one is a relay spacecraft, and one is an Earth ground station.
2. Start a timer. The sensor writes a made-up temperature and the time it was measured on a slip of paper. Pass it to the relay.
3. Close the link to Earth for 30 seconds. The relay holds the slip until the link opens, then passes it to the ground station.
4. The ground station writes down the time it received the slip and works out how old the reading is. Repeat with the link open from the start and compare.

The 30 seconds model a break in contact. Record both runs as a simulation. Ask what the station should show while it waits for a new reading.
