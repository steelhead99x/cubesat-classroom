# [M] Measure the full ASA rail frame

## Goal

A full-length ASA frame printed on the P1S has a recorded comparison with the full PLA frame.

## Plan

Use the ASA profile and lot from the coupon work. Compare rail straightness, shrinkage, twist, corner lift and surface condition after support removal.

## Required

- [ASA rail coupon](/quest/m0/asa-rail-coupon.md) - establishes whether the ASA print process can scale up.
- [Full PLA rail frame](/quest/m0/full-frame-fit.md) - provides the full-length PLA control.
