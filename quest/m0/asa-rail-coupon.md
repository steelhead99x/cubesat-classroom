# [S] Measure the ASA rail coupon

## Goal

A builder measures the ASA coupon against the same dimensions as PLA and records material-specific shrinkage, lift and support scars.

## Plan

Use the ASA vendor profile and record the printer process and filament lot. Retain raw measurements and identify any setting change needed before printing a full frame.

## Required

- [PLA rail coupon](/quest/m0/print-and-fit.md) - provides the measurement method and first material baseline.
