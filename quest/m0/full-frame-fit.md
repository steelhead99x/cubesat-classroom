# [M] Measure the full printed rail frame

## Goal

A full-length Rev B frame printed on a P1S has a recorded dimensional and visual inspection at each end and the middle.

## Plan

Measure rail width, overall width, rail length, twist and straightness. Use PLA for this first full frame. Record filament lot, orientation, supports and dimensional variation.

## Required

- [PLA rail coupon](/quest/m0/print-and-fit.md) - establishes a repeatable first print setup.
