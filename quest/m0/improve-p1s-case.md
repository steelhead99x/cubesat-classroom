# [S] Review a case improvement against the fit evidence

## Goal

Turn a measured problem into one reviewable change while keeping the requirements for the rest of the case visible.

## Plan

Choose one issue from the printed fit report. Record the baseline, the suspected cause, and how you will tell whether a change helped. Decide whether to change the print process or the [parametric CAD](/hardware/generate.py). Document the proposed setting or geometry change and have another builder review it. If CAD changes, regenerate the STL and P1S 3MF files and identify the new revision before reprinting. If the baseline meets the bench needs, record that conclusion and retain it for comparison.

## Required

- [Case assembly fit test](/quest/m0/test-p1s-case-fit.md) - provides the measured baseline and any defects.
