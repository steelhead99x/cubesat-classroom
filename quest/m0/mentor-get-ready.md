# [S] Mentor: get ready to guide a demo

## Goal

A teacher, guardian or mentor runs every mentor demo once on their own and agrees to the mentor rules before guiding anyone else.

## Plan

### Mentor rules

These apply to every mentor quest.

1. **100% participation.** The mentor stays for the whole demo, from setup to pack-up, and works alongside the learners. If the mentor has to leave, the demo stops until they come back. Every learner does each step with their own hands: nobody only watches.
2. **No login sharing with anyone under 13.** Never give a password, a sign-in code or a signed-in device to anyone under 13. If a step needs a sign-in, the mentor does that step. The demos below need no login at all.
3. **Safety first.** The mentor checks wiring before anything is powered, keeps batteries and soldering irons in adult hands, and follows a licensed operator for anything that transmits on amateur bands.
4. **No personal details.** Use crew nicknames. Do not collect names, schools, photos or contact details.
5. **Every level is welcome.** Explain jargon, answer beginner questions without hurry, and let experienced learners go deeper with the Professional notes in each lesson.

### Steps

1. Read the five rules above out loud to yourself or a co-mentor. Write "I agree" and your nickname in your mentor log.
2. Run the [practice ground station](https://cubesatclass.org/#demo) in a browser. Ask for a reading, take pictures and pause contact.
3. Install the local station with `bash docs/start.sh` and open the address it prints. Check that the reading, video and contact buttons work.
4. Read the mentor demos you plan to run, from start to finish, and gather what each one lists under **Bring**.
5. Note one step you think learners will find hard, and how you will explain it.

### Check your work

You can run each demo without the instructions open, and you know who to call if something breaks (a co-mentor, the teacher or the [contribution guide](/CONTRIBUTING.md)).
