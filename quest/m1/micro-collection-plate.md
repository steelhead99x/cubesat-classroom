# [M] Review the microscope collection plate proposal

## Goal

A teacher, a radio volunteer and a materials or optics reviewer each comment on the [collection plate proposal](/docs/micro-collection-plate.md). The crew records which measurement question to pursue first, an estimated image size per pass, and a desk-stage plan for adding a simulated microscope image to the classroom station.

## Plan

Start with the desk stage. Collect a short stored sequence of microscope images of a classroom dust slide, with exposure and focus settings, and propose the object format and metadata fields. Keep the bench, balloon and orbital stages as reviewed proposals until the desk stage is repeatable by a second group.
