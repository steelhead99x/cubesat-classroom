# [L] Design the printed chassis and rail interface

## Goal

Deliver a manufacturable P1S chassis drawing matched to the provider’s controlled rail, end-contact, dimensional and venting requirements.

## Plan

Use the verified deployer drawing and keep the rails and load-bearing chassis printable. Show datums, tolerances, contact surfaces, clearances and vent paths. Record deviations in the matrix.

## Required

- [Printed material and process](/quest/m1/printed-structure-qualification.md) - provides the design and manufacturing constraints.
- [Printed end-plate fit](/quest/m0/end-plate-fit.md) - documents the bench geometry and clearances.
