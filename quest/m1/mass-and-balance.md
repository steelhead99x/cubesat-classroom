# [M] Close the mass and center-of-mass budget

## Goal

Compare a stowed CAD and measured engineering-article mass and center of mass against the provider limits with explicit margins.

## Plan

Include the battery, mounts, retainers, harness, antennas and payload. Verify the controlling limit and use the provisional 1.33 kg screening ceiling while the provider requirement is unresolved.

## Required

- [Printed payload mounts](/quest/m1/printed-payload-mounts.md) - defines the internal arrangement.
- [Printed plate retainers](/quest/m1/printed-retainers.md) - provides the retention geometry and mass.
