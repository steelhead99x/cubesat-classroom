# [M] Prepare the crewed-flight battery safety evidence

## Goal

Identify the exact cell and pack and obtain a reviewed evidence plan for protection, wiring, containment and assigned battery tests.

## Plan

Use the provider's applicability decision for JSC 20793 and its forms. Record a parts list, schematic, test identifiers and exception status. Coordinate hazardous testing through an appropriate lab.

## Required

- [ISS compliance matrix](/quest/m1/iss-compliance-matrix.md) - identifies the provider and applicable battery requirements.
