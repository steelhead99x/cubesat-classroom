# [M] Design positive printed plate retainers

## Goal

Demonstrate an installable and inspectable printed retention feature for both end plates and document its bench retention and removal behavior.

## Plan

Begin with the Rev B plate fit. Build a small retainer coupon before iterating the whole frame. Record installation, access, unintended release and cycle observations. Coordinate the final flight design against the provider's retention requirement.

## Required

- [Printed end-plate fit](/quest/m0/end-plate-fit.md) - defines the mating geometry and demonstrated clearances.
