# [M] Dry-fit the pedal stack and panel

## Goal

Verify the panel parts, connector mating and board stack in a Hammond 1590N1 enclosure.

## Plan

Use the [panel BOM](/hardware/sdr-receiver/pedal/panel-bom.md) and 1:1 drill template. Check the J11/J1 pin-one alignment, effective header insertion, standoffs, OLED and shaft clearances, switch slot, lid and cable bends. Check DC jack isolation with the enclosure unpowered. Capture dimensions and any drawing changes.

## Waiting

- Maintainer (PC): Make the 1590N1 enclosure and representative panel and stacking parts available for a documented dry fit.
