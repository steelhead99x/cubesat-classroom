# [M] Review pedal input protection and chassis wiring

## Goal

Close the 9 V protection, USB backfeed and chassis return findings before ordering or wiring the pedal.

## Plan

Confirm Q1 polarity and gate clamp behavior. D2 is upstream of F1, so verify sustained clamp current and the approved adapter or revise the protection. Check the D6 VBUS path, possible I/O backfeed, the isolated center-negative jack and the intended SMA, audio and standoff bonds. If the source changes, regenerate both the relevant checks and fabrication files. Record physical measurements during prototype bring-up.
