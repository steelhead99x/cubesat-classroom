# [M] Agree the printed material and process

## Goal

Record the provider-reviewed material and manufacturing process for the all-printed structure, plus the evidence and change controls needed to qualify that process.

## Plan

Agree on the exact filament and lot tracking, machine and nozzle, orientation, settings, post-processing, witness coupons and inspection. Attach material and process reviews to the compliance matrix. Do not record a bench filament as accepted for flight based on print fit alone.

## Required

- [ISS compliance matrix](/quest/m1/iss-compliance-matrix.md) - lists the material and process requirements.
- [ASA rail coupon](/quest/m0/asa-rail-coupon.md) - provides initial P1S print evidence.
