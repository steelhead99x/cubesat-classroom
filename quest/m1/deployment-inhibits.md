# [L] Verify the deployment inhibit design

## Goal

Record a schematic and bench evidence that independent hardware inhibits and post-deployment timing meet the assigned power and transmission requirements.

## Plan

Test stowed, partially released, deployed and relevant fault states using bench equipment. Keep the MoQ payload separate from the safety chain.

## Required

- [ISS compliance matrix](/quest/m1/iss-compliance-matrix.md) - identifies the provider’s electrical and inhibit requirements.
