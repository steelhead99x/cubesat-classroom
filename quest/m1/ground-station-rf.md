# [L] Specify the first two-node conducted RF test

## Goal

A radio mentor documents a reproducible SubGHz conducted bench using identified modems and the paired relay. The test records link margin assumptions, fresh telemetry, contact loss, camera goodput and recovery. Identify which S-band or X-band ground equipment, antenna and tracking work remain after the bench.

## Plan

Keep receiver identity, waveform, timing and loss evidence with the result. Keep real RF, the software relay bench and flight qualification in separate evidence records.

- [Two-relay lesson](/quest/m0/repeat-the-station-lesson.md) - establishes a known classroom baseline and review procedure.
