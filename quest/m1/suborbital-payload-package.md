# [M] Draft the suborbital payload package

## Goal

Produce a draft Payload Information Package for the classroom sensor kit that a host reviewer can mark up, with every unknown listed as a question.

## Required

- [Predict the spaceflight](/quest/m0/predict-the-spaceflight.md) gives the timeline and sampling needs.
- [Balloon bench log](/quest/m0/balloon-ground-log.md) gives a bench record with a deliberate gap.

## Plan

1. Read the requirements summary in the [suborbital flight plan](/docs/suborbital-flight-plan.md#what-the-providers-guide-asks-of-a-payload) and the provider's current Payload User's Guide.
2. Write one page for each: parts list with models; dimensions and measured mass; battery make, capacity and protection; the single power switch; radios and how they are disabled; materials, including printed parts; hazards and how each is contained; switch-on, switch-off and data hand-back steps.
3. Attach evidence: a full-length bench log, the measured mass, a photo of the switch, and a radio-off check witnessed by a mentor.
4. List open questions from the flight plan, and add your own.
5. Have an experienced mentor, engineer or teacher review it. Someone who has flown a hosted payload is ideal. Record their decision and changes.

A draft that follows the public guide does not establish acceptance or a flight slot. Teachers in Space and the flight provider decide.
