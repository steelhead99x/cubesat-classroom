# [L] Verify printed rail wear and debris

## Goal

Submit sliding wear and debris observations against the assigned provider criteria.

## Plan

Use an agreed rail specimen and mating surface or approved fixture. Record contact damage, particle results, dimensional change and any written exception.

## Required

- [ISS verification plan](/quest/m1/iss-verification-plan.md) - defines rail test articles and criteria.
- [Rail hardness and finish](/quest/m2/rail-surface.md) - provides the initial condition of the rail contact surfaces.
