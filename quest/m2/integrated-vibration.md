# [L] Verify integrated vibration

## Goal

An agreed test article completes the assigned vibration verification with traceable pre-test and post-test inspection and functional results.

## Plan

Record fixture, article configuration, axes, levels and anomalies. Check the printed retainers and mounts after exposure.

## Required

- [ISS verification plan](/quest/m1/iss-verification-plan.md) - specifies tests and acceptance criteria.
- [Pre-test deployer fit](/quest/m2/pretest-deployer-fit.md) - provides a mechanical baseline.
- [Layer-direction strength](/quest/m2/printed-strength.md) - provides process-level strength evidence.
- [Creep verification](/quest/m2/printed-creep.md) - provides evidence of dimensional stability under load.
