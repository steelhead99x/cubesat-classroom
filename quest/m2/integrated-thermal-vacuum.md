# [L] Verify thermal and vacuum behavior

## Goal

Submit assigned thermal, pressure and vacuum verification evidence for the printed assembly and its battery and payload configuration.

## Plan

Measure relevant dimensions and function before and after exposure. Keep outgassing evidence linked. Include bakeout only when the assigned requirements call for it.

## Required

- [ISS verification plan](/quest/m1/iss-verification-plan.md) - defines thermal, pressure and vacuum requirements.
- [Battery safety verification](/quest/m2/battery-verification.md) - provides the accepted battery configuration.
- [Outgassing and materials evidence](/quest/m2/material-outgassing.md) - closes the material review input.
