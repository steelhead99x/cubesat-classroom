# [L] Complete the provider’s deployer fit check

## Goal

Record the provider’s fit-check disposition on the agreed assembly after its required environmental exposure.

## Plan

Submit dimensional inspection, rail contact evidence and measured mass properties. Record rework and retest requirements before delivery.

## Required

- [Integrated vibration](/quest/m2/integrated-vibration.md) - provides vibration exposure evidence.
- [Integrated shock](/quest/m2/integrated-shock.md) - provides shock verification or applicability disposition.
- [Thermal and vacuum verification](/quest/m2/integrated-thermal-vacuum.md) - provides environmental exposure evidence.
- [Rail surface and debris](/quest/m2/rail-wear-debris.md) - provides the provider’s rail evidence.
