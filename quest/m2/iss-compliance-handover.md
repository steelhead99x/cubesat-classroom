# [L] Submit the ISS qualification evidence

## Goal

Deliver the configuration-controlled compliance package and obtain a written disposition for the chassis, printed rails and any open exceptions.

## Plan

Attach inspection and test results, the battery and inhibit records, applicable EMC verification, and status of the required spectrum and debris evidence. Record each outstanding item and who owns it.

## Required

- [Provider fit check](/quest/m2/deployer-fit-check.md) - records fit on the agreed assembly.
- [Deployment inhibit design](/quest/m1/deployment-inhibits.md) - provides power and timing verification.
- [Battery safety verification](/quest/m2/battery-verification.md) - provides the battery safety record.
- [Integrated EMC](/quest/m2/integrated-emc.md) - provides verification or applicability disposition.
