# [M] Measure printed rail hardness and finish

## Goal

Submit measured hardness and surface finish for the rail contact faces against the provider criteria.

## Plan

Use the specified measurement methods on the controlled print and post-processing configuration. Record variance and any exception.

## Required

- [ISS verification plan](/quest/m1/iss-verification-plan.md) - defines the surface measurement methods.
- [Printed chassis and rails](/quest/m1/flight-rail-cad.md) - identifies the controlled contact faces.
