# [M] Record the pre-test deployer fit

## Goal

Document the assembled test article’s baseline dimensions, contact geometry and initial fit in a provider-agreed fixture.

## Plan

Record the fixture identity, article revision, rail measurements and inspection results. Follow the provider’s procedure for whether this is a formal provider fit check or an engineering baseline.

## Required

- [ISS verification plan](/quest/m1/iss-verification-plan.md) - identifies the agreed article and fixture.
- [Mass and center-of-mass budget](/quest/m1/mass-and-balance.md) - identifies the assembled configuration.
