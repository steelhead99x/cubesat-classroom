CSC-R1P, the first pedal, has no HaLow; this is the Rev B plan. The chart is on a log scale. Lab boards measured 2.7–3.3 Mbit/s at 4 MHz, centimetres apart. Raw I/Q at 192 kHz, 16 bits, two channels is 6.1 Mbit/s, so it can't go over HaLow; I/Q decimated to 48 kHz, Opus voice and the stats track all fit. The Opus rate is a typical voice setting, and the stats figure is one roughly 600-byte snapshot a second. The MM6108 is the chip the project's Rust HaLow firmware already drives. Bluetooth LE is for setup only. HaLow carries ordinary IP, so the same MoQ publisher runs over it unchanged.
