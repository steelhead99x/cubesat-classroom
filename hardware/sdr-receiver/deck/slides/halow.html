The pedal publishes once over QUIC/MoQ. The classroom relay or moq.pro distributes to every viewer.

Raw I/Q doesn’t fit HaLow

Lab boards measured 2.7–3.3 Mbit/s at 4 MHz, centimetres apart. Raw I/Q needs about 6.1.

CSC-R1P, the first pedal, has no HaLow; this is the Rev B plan. The MM6108 is the chip the project's Rust HaLow firmware already drives. Bluetooth LE is for setup only. HaLow carries ordinary IP, so the same MoQ publisher runs over it unchanged.
