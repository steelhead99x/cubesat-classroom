Pedal · RF review

Radios and a receiver in one metal box

1

No antenna works inside. ANT and the XIAO’s Wi-Fi come out on wall SMA and RP-SMA bulkheads by U.FL pigtails; Rev B’s HaLow gets its own.

2

HaLow shouts into the receiver. Receive antenna on the mast by coax, 600 MHz low-pass before the LNA, shield cans. Measure desense first.

3

The box rings near 2.7 GHz. Close to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi: antennas outside, a scrap of absorber.

4

Every cable is an antenna. Filter USB, 9 V and audio at the wall; check the 2.1 MHz buck’s harmonics on both bands.

5

Ground loops. Amp plus laptop means two ground paths: isolate the audio output.

6

Two transmitters at once (Rev B). HaLow with Wi-Fi is a co-location test for certification.

Desk review · estimates and calculations · not a substitute for an RF engineer

Items 1, 2 and 4 shape the layout and the enclosure drilling, so they are decided first. Lid openings and heat are minor and are covered in the Rev B plan.
