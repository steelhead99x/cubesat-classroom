Why build our own

The receiver we planned on is gone

End of line

RTL-SDR Blog V4

Its R828D tuner is no longer made. The V4’s end of line was announced on 14 May 2026.

NDA only

RTL2832U and Rafael tuners

Their datasheets go only to partners under NDA, so we can’t design with them in the open.

Our choice

Public parts only

Every chip on CSC-R1 has a public datasheet. Anyone can check, change and build it.

The station plan used an RTL-SDR Blog V4. Its tuner chip ran out, and the alternatives are NDA-only. So we designed our own receiver from parts anyone can read about.
