Direct conversion

An ADF4351 synthesizer with a 25 MHz TCXO tunes the demodulator straight to baseband for 144–146 and 435–438 MHz.

192 kHz of bandwidth

About 6 Mbit/s of I/Q at 16 bits: plenty for satellite voice, packet, 9600-baud data and their Doppler shift.

Direct conversion keeps the part count low. The ADC is an audio converter with programmable gain, which probably removes the need for a separate baseband amplifier.
