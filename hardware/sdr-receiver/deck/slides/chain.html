Green: RF from the antenna.
Amber: the local oscillator.
Blue: baseband I and Q.

Direct conversion. The synthesizer tunes the demodulator straight to baseband for 144–146 and 435–438 MHz.

192 kHz of I/Q, about 6 Mbit/s at 16 bits.

Follow the dots: green RF comes in from the antenna, is amplified and filtered, and meets the amber local oscillator in the demodulator, which turns it into blue I and Q at baseband for the ADC. Direct conversion keeps the part count low. The ADC is an audio converter with programmable gain, which probably removes the need for a separate baseband amplifier.
