The markers follow the signal: antenna on the left edge, amplifier, filters, demodulator and synthesizer across the middle, the ADC above them, and the XIAO sockets top right. All parts are on top. Layer 2 is an unbroken ground plane. The RF path is routed by hand; the rest by a small router written for this board. Order it as 1.6 mm with 0.21 mm prepreg under the top layer. The empty top-left area showed the radio could be smaller: the pedal build, CSC-R1P, puts the same circuit on a 54 by 104 millimetre board.
