12 bench quests and scripts: hardware/sdr-receiver/bench · quest/README.md “CSC-R1 receiver bench”

The trick is to compare, not to calibrate. Every quest measures something twice with the same dongle and gain: load versus antenna, HaLow off versus on, noise straight through versus through the filter. The difference is trustworthy even when the absolute number isn't. Six of the quests need no CSC-R1 board, so a crew can start the day an RTL-SDR V4 arrives. The $250 is an estimate for the stand-ins plus a load, attenuators, a tee, cables and a splitter; it leaves out the XIAO and HaLow boards. Safety: bias tee off, no transmitter ever into a dongle, and a licensed operator approves test frequencies on 145 and 437 MHz.
