CSC-R1
satellite receiver

An open, receive-only software-defined radio for the 2 m and 70 cm satellite bands, built from parts with public datasheets. A dev kit, a pedal-shaped build, and a full simulation in the station, website and app.

Desk design · pedal placed, routing unfinished · not built · not measured · 27 November 2026

CSC-R1 is the receiver for the classroom ground station. Everything in this deck is desk design: the boards are designed and checked by scripts, but nothing has been built or measured yet. The simulation runs today and is labelled desk simulation everywhere.
