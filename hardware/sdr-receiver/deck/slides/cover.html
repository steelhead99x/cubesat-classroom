CSC-R1
satellite receiver

An open, receive-only software-defined radio for the 2 m and 70 cm satellite bands, built from parts with public datasheets. First a dev kit, then a stompbox with HaLow.

Desk design · not built · not measured · November 2026

CSC-R1 is the receiver for the classroom ground station. Everything in this deck is desk design: the board is designed and rule-checked, but nothing has been built or measured yet.
