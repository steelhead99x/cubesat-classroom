Rev B

The satellite stompbox

The same receiver in a 125B guitar-pedal box. Turn TUNE, ride GAIN, stomp to mute, plug the OUT jack into an amp, and hear the ISS go over.

Pedal conventions

Signal right to left, 1/4 inch OUT, 9 V center-negative, knobs up top, footswitch at the bottom.

Works standalone

OLED, encoder and pots on a lid board. No laptop needed.

Made to be hacked

Open hardware, Qwiic port and a badge add-on header inside.

Rev B is a plan, not a design. The faceplate concept is on the CSC-R1 pedal faceplate canvas.
