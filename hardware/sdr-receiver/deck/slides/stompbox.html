The pedal

The satellite stompbox

The same receiver in a 125B guitar-pedal box. Turn TUNE, ride GAIN, stomp ENGAGE, plug OUT into an amp and hear the ISS go over. The first pedal, CSC-R1P, only listens.

Pedal conventions

Signal right to left, 1/4 inch OUT, 9 V center-negative, knobs up top, footswitch at the bottom.

Works standalone

OLED, encoder and pots on a control board under the face. No laptop needed.

Receive first

No HaLow on CSC-R1P. HaLow and the power monitors wait for Rev B, after the desense tests.

We respun Rev A to fit the pedal box, so the first board built is already the pedal. HaLow moved to Rev B so the first pedal is a receiver only: its one transmitter is the XIAO's own Wi-Fi, which the firmware controls.
