CSC-R1P · status

Two boards in a 125B box

Main board · 54 × 104 mm

Rev A’s radio, unchanged and checked part by part, plus 9 V protection, a 2.1 MHz buck and a PCM5102A audio out.

Control board · 54 × 72 mm

OLED, TUNE encoder, GAIN and LEVEL pots, footswitch and toggle. The placer checks all 8 stacking pins.

Fixed this round

Self-crossing outline at a lid-post notch, design rules lost after routing, a track through a switch pin.

Not finished

The template is printed 1:1 and taped to the box for drilling. Placement is clean: no overlaps and no design-rule errors apart from the unrouted connections.
