# [S] Validate the Rev A case in Bambu Studio

## Goal

Slice all three Rev A parts with the settings in the [print guide](/hardware/p1s/README.md) so another builder can repeat the same slice.

## Plan

1. Open each 3MF from the [print guide](/hardware/p1s/README.md) with the settings listed there. Don't rotate them.
2. In Preview, check that the shell sits floor down and the lid sits tabs up, and that the screw holes, cable slot, tabs and camera hole look clean.
3. Record your Bambu Studio version, plate, filament preset, any setting you changed and why, and any warning. Save a preview screenshot of each part.
