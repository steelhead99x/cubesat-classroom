# [S] Measure what the camera lid blocks

## Goal

Find out whether the 18 mm opening in the Rev A lid reduces the usable view of the intended camera.

## Plan

1. Mount the intended camera securely on the bench at its proposed position. Put a grid target at a recorded distance. Take a reference image with the lid off.
2. Seat the lid without moving the camera or target. Take a second image with the same camera settings. Count visible grid columns and rows or mark blocked corners in both images.
3. Repeat after removing and reseating the lid. Publish the placement, target distance, and both grid counts or cropped target-only images. If there is clipping, note whether moving the camera or changing the opening would address it. Record a mock camera as a fit reference only.

## Required

- [Printed case assembly test](/archive/quest/m0/test-p1s-case-fit.md) - provides the fitted lid and the tested camera position or mock-up.
